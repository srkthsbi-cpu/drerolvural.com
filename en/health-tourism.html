Frequently asked questions

Can the first assessment be online?

The first consultation and review of available records may begin online. A face-to-face clinical assessment may be required before a surgical decision.

How long should I stay in Türkiye?

There is no single duration for everyone. The stay depends on examinations, treatment, recovery and clinical recommendations.
